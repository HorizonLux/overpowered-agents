# Monthly cash-flow summary and accountant hand-off

Read this when you write a monthly cash-flow summary (Step 4 of `SKILL.md`) or an accountant hand-off checklist (Step 5). It gives both templates and the checks to run.

Every figure in a summary comes from the user. You add, subtract and compare; you never estimate, round away or "smooth" a figure. A missing figure is `[TO CONFIRM]`.

## What to ask for

1. The month, and the account or accounts it covers.
2. The opening balance at the start of the month.
3. Money received during the month (a total, or the lines).
4. Money paid out during the month (a total, or the lines; the categorised expenses from Step 2 work well).
5. Invoices sent and not yet paid: customer, invoice number, amount, due date.
6. Bills due soon: who, amount, due date. The user says how far ahead to look.
7. Optional: the actual closing balance from the bank, to check against.

## Template

```
CASH-FLOW SUMMARY: [month YYYY]          Account(s): [names, last four characters only]
Currency: [currency]                     Basis: [cash / accrual / TO CONFIRM]

| Line                          | Amount      | Working                          |
|-------------------------------|-------------|----------------------------------|
| Opening balance               | [given]     | from the user                    |
| Money in                      | [sum]       | [line + line + ...]              |
| Money out                     | [sum]       | [line + line + ...]              |
| Closing balance (worked out)  | [result]    | opening + in - out               |
| Closing balance (bank)        | [given]     | from the user, or TO CONFIRM     |
| Difference                    | [result]    | bank - worked out; 0 if they match |

STILL OWED TO YOU
| Customer   | Invoice | Amount  | Due date   | Past due? |
| [name]     | [no.]   | [given] | [given]    | [yes / no] |
Total still owed: [sum]

BILLS DUE BY [date the user chose]
| Who        | Amount  | Due date   |
| [name]     | [given] | [given]    |
Total bills due: [sum]

WHAT STANDS OUT (description only, no advice)
- [for example: invoice [no.] is past its due date]
- [for example: bills due before [date] add up to more than the worked-out closing balance]

TO CONFIRM
- [every missing or unmatched figure]

DRAFT: check every figure against your bank and your records.
```

## Checks before you hand it over

- Every sum is written out in the Working column and adds up.
- If the bank's closing balance differs from the worked-out one, the difference is shown and listed under TO CONFIRM. Common reasons to suggest checking: a missing line, a line counted twice, a payment still pending, a fee, or a currency conversion. Do not adjust any figure to make it match.
- "Past due" compares the due date with the date the user gives as today; if you do not know today's date, ask.
- "What stands out" describes the figures. It does not recommend a loan, an investment, a payment plan or a tax move.
- A forecast, if the user asks for one, is labelled as the user's assumptions, in their words, line by line.

## Accountant hand-off checklist

Ask the user first for the accountant's own list. If they have one, follow it and add the missing items below it. If not, use this skeleton.

```
HAND-OFF CHECKLIST: [month or accounting year]      For: [accountant's name]

RECORDS TO SEND
[ ] Categorised expenses for the period (Step 2 output)
[ ] Invoices sent, numbers [first] to [last], and any credit notes
[ ] Bank and card statements for every business account, [start] to [end]
[ ] Receipts for the period
[ ] [anything on the accountant's own list]

MISSING
[ ] Receipts missing: [date, description, amount as given], one per line
[ ] Unsure lines: [date, description, amount, the question]
[ ] Invoice numbers missing from the sequence: [numbers], with the user's explanation

QUESTIONS FOR THE ACCOUNTANT
1. [flagged line, written as a plain question]
2. [any tax question the user asked, which this skill does not answer]

DATES
- Accounting year: [start] to [end]
- Deadlines: [TO CONFIRM: from your tax authority or accountant]

YOU DO
1. Fill the gaps above.  2. Send the records to your accountant yourself.
```
