# Sources and exact quotes

Every rule in `SKILL.md` and `technical-checklist.md` rests on one of these pages. The codes match the Sources list in `SKILL.md`.

- **Last updated:** the date the page itself shows ("undated" when it shows only a copyright year).
- **Checked:** the day the quote was read on the page.
- A quote marked "(part)" is the opening or a part of a longer sentence; nothing is spliced.

Google changes these pages. Before relying on a rule for an important decision, open the page and look for the sentence. If it is gone or says something else, follow the page, not this file.

## What these sources do not settle

- **Search volumes** for any phrase. No free Google source gives exact counts; Keyword Planner gives estimates [H7], Trends gives relative interest [H5]. This skill therefore treats every volume as an unverified estimate.
- **Ranking effects** of any single change. Google publishes no such numbers in these pages, and no one can guarantee a ranking [G4].
- **Rules outside Google Search.** Other search engines publish their own guidelines; this skill does not cover them.
